# 5 Mistakes to Avoid When Buying Your First Original Watch in Pakistan

*By [Beater.pk](https://beater.pk) — 100% original watches, delivered across Pakistan with cash on delivery.*

Buying your first original watch should be exciting. In Pakistan, though, first-time buyers make the same handful of mistakes — and most of them end with money wasted on a replica, or the wrong watch entirely. Here are the five to avoid.

## 1. Chasing the lowest price

If a "brand new original" G-Shock or Citizen is offered for a few thousand rupees, it is not original. Original watches in Pakistan start from around PKR 12,900 and run up to about PKR 305,000 for premium pieces. That price floor is the single most reliable replica tell there is. A deal that ignores it is not a bargain — it is a copy with a brand name printed on it.

## 2. Ignoring the warranty card and the box

An original watch arrives with two things a replica seller can rarely fake convincingly together: its warranty card and its genuine brand box. Ask for both before you pay, and walk away from any seller who waves them off as unimportant. They are not packaging extras — they are the proof of what you bought, and what makes service and resale possible later.

## 3. Buying from a seller who cannot say where the watch came from

"Original" is a sourcing claim, not a feeling. A trustworthy seller states plainly that its watches are sourced from authorised dealers. If a seller cannot answer that question, or answers it with vague assurances, treat that as your answer. This matters more than any single product photo, because photos of originals are exactly what replica sellers use.

## 4. Choosing on looks alone, not on how you will wear it

A watch you love in a photo can disappoint on your wrist. Before choosing a brand, think about your week. If you want one watch for everything, including rough daily use, a [G-Shock](https://beater.pk/collections/casio-g-shock) is built for exactly that. If you want something dressier for work and occasions, [Casio Edifice](https://beater.pk/collections/casio-edifice) or [Citizen](https://beater.pk/collections/citizen) — Citizen's Eco-Drive runs on light, so there are no battery changes — will serve you better. Match the watch to your life first, and the design second.

## 5. Skipping the after-sales basics

Before ordering, check four things: cash on delivery nationwide, a replacement window (seven days is a fair standard), delivery time (2–5 working days is normal for a stocked retailer), and a real way to reach the seller — a physical address, a phone number, an email. Prepaid orders should earn you something in return, such as a discount and free shipping. A seller with no address and no phone number is a seller you cannot find if something goes wrong.

## The safe way to buy your first original

Start with a seller who ticks every box above, pick a brand that fits your daily life, and insist on the warranty card and genuine box. Do that, and your first original watch will be exactly that — original.
