# Original Watches in Pakistan — A Buyer's Guide

*By [Beater.pk](https://beater.pk) — 100% original watches, delivered across Pakistan with cash on delivery.*

Buying a genuine watch in Pakistan takes a little care: popular models from Casio, G-Shock and Citizen are widely copied. This short guide covers what to check, what things cost, and where to buy with confidence.

## The 3 checks that catch most fakes

1. **Model number** — every genuine watch has a full model number (e.g. GA-2100-1A1). Search it: the watch in front of you must match it exactly.
2. **Weight and finish** — originals feel dense, with crisp dial printing and clean caseback engraving. Copies feel hollow and look blurry up close.
3. **Written guarantee** — buy from a seller who guarantees 100% original in writing and offers cash on delivery, so you inspect before you pay.

## What genuine watches cost (Pakistan, 2026)

- **Under PKR 25,000** — entry-level original Casio digital and quartz models.
- **PKR 25,000–50,000** — the sweet spot: G-Shock DW-5600 and GA-700, the GA-2100 "CasiOak", Casio Edifice chronographs, entry Citizen quartz.
- **PKR 50,000–100,000** — Citizen Eco-Drive, Citizen Tsuyosa automatics, premium G-Shock models.
